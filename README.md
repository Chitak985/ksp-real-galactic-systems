# Real Galacitc Systems

This is a KSP planet pack (more like a universe pack) that adds systems found in the Milky Way galaxy and many other galaxies too! Join my modding server for updates about this and my other mods: [https://discord.gg/qgdEU2X6Xj](https://discord.gg/qgdEU2X6Xj)

## Installation
To download the mod, do Download ZIP and put the RealGalacticSystems folder into your GameData.

Do **NOT** download the release, it's for history purposes and is utter stupidity and bugginess.

## Important info (READ THIS!!!!!)
- If you plan on going to literally anywhere outside the home system (if you zoom out while centered on the home star, the moment you see other stars around you is the absolute limit), YOU MUST ENABLE HEAT CHEATS! This is because KSP breaks down at these distances and applies Infinity kelvin temperatures to everything, capable of vaporizing the entire universe instantly.
- EVERYTHING IS REAL SCALE! If you can't go 100 times the speed of light, don't even bother going anywhere other than the closest stars.
- Make sure to not set your time warp to quadrillions and warp until a star, KSP will crash when saving after about 100,000 years (not actually tested).
- The home system is not changed in any way and is placed at where Sol is in the Milky Way galaxy, which is why real-scale Sol will work the best with this mod.
- Some planet mods **do** work correctly, however they need to be tested first. Please open an issue or tell me in my modding server if you find a mod that is compatible or incompatible. (link above)
- All data comes from online stellar databases, however the farther you go from the home system the more estimates and approximations there are. Also names become IDs at some point
- Please give me feedback, this mod is completely untested due to it being insanely hard to go anywhere and idk if you even can. (you sure can with cheats)

## Dependencies
- Kopernicus Expansion Continued-er
- ModuleManager
- Singularity (Black hole rendering)
- Sigma Loading Screens (Awesome loading screens)
- Scaled Decorator (Pulsar jets)
- GalacticDisk (Disks around galaxies and a few other things)
- Scatterer (Sunflares and reduced brightness in intergalactic space)

## Supported mods
None for now.

## Compatible mods
Most system replacers, the home system is not modified in any way.

## Incompatible mods
Most interstellar mods, they work but the objects move with the home star.
